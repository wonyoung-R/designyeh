# Pricing policy — 2026-09-17

The owner's instructions set these prices; they are not market benchmarks or prices recommended by the source below.

## Public offer

- Basic landing page: KRW 300,000 excluding VAT and third-party service costs. Includes consultation, page structure, basic setup, responsive implementation, checks and deployment for one page with up to nine sections. Roughly one hour of internal production is not the customer-facing billing unit or a promised consultation duration.
- Deliver the agreed basic landing-page finished version within 12 elapsed hours of the agreed start time, after required copy/images and scope are confirmed. Communicate that timestamp. Admin/DB/custom work has a separately agreed schedule. Changed materials or scope require an updated agreement. Delivery does not remove acceptance checks or the existing revision terms.
- Initial consultation: approximately 15 minutes, free. Assess business scale, content types, users/permissions, integrations and workflows to provide an estimate. Any further paid analysis or proof of concept is separately explained and agreed before starting.
- Additional development: starting estimating rate KRW 75,000/hour. Negotiate a lower rate for larger amounts of work and longer planned engagements; agree rate, estimated effort, total price and completion criteria in advance. This is not automatically a declining rate based on elapsed calendar time. Clear scopes use agreed totals; uncertain scopes use separately estimated analysis/validation/delivery phases.
- Customer-site third-party costs: entirely customer-funded, disclosed before use. Monthly management total = management labor (from KRW 30,000/site/month, VAT excluded) + recurring subscription costs at cost. Itemize both, and do not charge again for subscriptions the customer pays directly. Agree usage charges, budget caps, annual/one-time fees, and cost-change communication.
- Existing 30-day/two simple revisions and 24-hour revision-response terms remain applicable as published. Development defect correction is separate from additional feature requests.

[UK Digital, Data and Technology Playbook — Payment mechanism and pricing approach](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook-html#payment-mechanism-and-pricing-approach), accessed 2026-09-17.

Applied principles: tie the price to agreed deliverables, choose a pricing mechanism according to scope certainty, and explain recognized charges and lifecycle costs. This public-sector guidance is a conceptual reference; it does not prescribe designYEH's prices, deadlines or customer terms.
